EPI-USE America, Inc. SMS Terms

These terms describe the optional SMS reminders used by the EPI-USE America, Inc. Billable Hours Tracker.

SMS Signup Form SMS Privacy

Last Updated

July 10, 2026

Program Description

EPI-USE America, Inc. uses SMS to request weekly billable-hours forecasts from opted-in consultants and to send follow-up reminders when a response has not been received.

Optional Consent

SMS consent is optional and is not required to participate in the Billable Hours Tracker process. You may use the tracker process without receiving SMS reminders.

How to Opt In

You may opt in by checking the optional SMS consent box on the SMS Signup Form, or by texting START, YES, or UNSTOP to +1 989 864 2355, the EPI-USE America, Inc. SMS number.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies, but messages are typically sent weekly with occasional follow-up reminders.

Costs

Message and data rates may apply.

Opt Out

You can cancel SMS messages at any time by replying STOP. After you send STOP, you may receive a final confirmation message and will no longer receive messages from this SMS program.

Help

Reply HELP for help. You may also contact your EPI-USE manager or administrator for support.

Privacy

Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. SMS opt-in data and consent are not sold, rented, or shared for marketing. See the SMS Privacy Policy.
