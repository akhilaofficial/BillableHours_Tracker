EPI-USE America, Inc. SMS Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 10, 2026

This SMS Privacy Policy explains how EPI-USE America, Inc. handles mobile phone numbers and SMS message information for the billable-hours forecasting SMS program.

Information We Collect

We collect consultant names, mobile phone numbers, SMS replies, message delivery information, consent records, and billable-hours forecast responses submitted by SMS.

How We Use SMS Information

SMS information is used only to send billable-hours forecasting messages, receive consultant replies, send follow-up reminders, and maintain internal workforce planning records.

No Sharing for Marketing

Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. SMS opt-in data and consent are not sold, rented, or shared for marketing.

Message Frequency and Rates

Message frequency varies, but messages are typically sent weekly, with occasional follow-up reminders. Message and data rates may apply.

Choices

Reply STOP to opt out of SMS messages. Reply HELP for help.

Terms

See the SMS Terms, SMS Opt-In Instructions, and SMS Signup Form.
