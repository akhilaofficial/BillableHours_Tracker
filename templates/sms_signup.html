Billable Hours SMS Opt-In

Use this page to join optional EPI-USE America, Inc. text reminders for weekly billable-hours forecasting. SMS consent is optional and is not a condition of your work or any transaction.

What you will receive

Low-volume reminders asking for your expected weekly billable hours. Reply with a number such as 40. Reply HELP for instructions or STOP to opt out.

How It Works

Simple, optional, auditable

  • Your manager or administrator provides this opt-in link.
  • You enter your name and U.S. mobile number.
  • The consent checkbox starts unchecked and must be selected by you.
  • Your opt-in is recorded with timestamp, source, and consent text.
  • You can opt out anytime by replying STOP.
