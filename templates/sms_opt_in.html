EPI-USE America, Inc. SMS Opt-In Instructions

Last updated: July 10, 2026

This SMS program is for EPI-USE America, Inc. consultants who need to submit weekly billable-hours forecasts for internal workforce planning.

How to Opt In

To opt in, text START, YES, or UNSTOP to +1 989 864 2355, the EPI-USE America, Inc. SMS number.

Confirmation Message

After opting in, you will receive a confirmation message from EPI-USE America, Inc. confirming that you are enrolled in SMS messages for billable-hours forecasting and follow-up reminders.

Confirmation message: EPI-USE America, Inc.: You are opted in to receive SMS messages for billable-hours forecasting and follow-up reminders. Message frequency varies, typically weekly. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help. Reply STOP to opt out.

Message Frequency and Rates

Message frequency varies, but messages are typically sent weekly, with occasional follow-up reminders. Message and data rates may apply.

Opt Out and Help

Reply STOP to opt out. Reply HELP for help.
