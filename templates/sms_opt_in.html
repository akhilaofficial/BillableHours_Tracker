EPI-USE America, Inc. SMS Opt-In Instructions

Last updated: July 10, 2026

This SMS program is for EPI-USE America, Inc. consultants who need to submit weekly billable-hours forecasts for internal workforce planning.

Website Opt-In

Visit https://billable-hours-tracker.onrender.com/sms-signup, enter your name and mobile phone number, review the optional SMS consent language, check the unchecked SMS consent box if you want SMS reminders, and submit the form. SMS consent is optional and is not required to participate in the Billable Hours Tracker process.

Keyword Opt-In

To opt in by text, send START, YES, or UNSTOP to +1 989 864 2355, the EPI-USE America, Inc. SMS number.

Confirmation Message

Opt Out and Help
