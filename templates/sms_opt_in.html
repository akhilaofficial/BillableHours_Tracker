Application Purpose

Weekly forecast collection

EPI-USE America, Inc. uses this application to track expected consultant billable hours by reporting week. Administrators can send reminders, review replies, and export weekly or monthly summaries.

  • Forecast requests Consultants receive low-volume operational messages asking for a numeric forecast response.
  • Internal use Responses are used for workforce planning and reporting. This program is not for marketing or promotions.
  • Optional SMS SMS reminders are optional. Message frequency varies, typically weekly. Message and data rates may apply.
