EPI-USE America, Inc.

Billable Hours Tracker SMS Program

This application helps EPI-USE America, Inc. collect weekly consultant billable-hours forecasts for internal workforce planning. SMS reminders are optional and are used only to request forecast responses and send follow-up reminders.

Open SMS Signup
Text reply format

When you receive a reminder, reply with your expected billable hours, such as 40. Reply HELP for help or STOP to opt out.

SMS Opt-In Flow

How SMS reminders work

  • 1. Open the signup form Enter your name and mobile number on the SMS signup page.
  • 2. Review the consent language SMS consent is optional and is not required to participate in the Billable Hours Tracker process.
  • 3. Choose whether to opt in Check the SMS consent box only if you want to receive text reminders.
  • 4. Reply to reminders If opted in, reply with a numeric forecast when a weekly reminder is sent.
